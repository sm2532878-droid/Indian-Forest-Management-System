Tourist Advisory

Guidelines for Visitors to Indian Forests, National Parks & Wildlife Sanctuaries

Purpose of Tourist Advisory

The Tourist Advisory section provides official guidelines and safety information for visitors travelling to forests, national parks, tiger reserves, and wildlife sanctuaries across India.

These advisories are issued to ensure visitor safety, protect wildlife, and promote responsible and sustainable eco-tourism practices.

General Guidelines for All Tourists

  • Carry valid government-issued identification at all times
  • Entry permitted only through authorized forest gates
  • Follow instructions issued by forest officials and guides
  • Respect wildlife and maintain safe distance
  • Strictly follow park timings and rules

Safety Advisory

Forest areas are natural habitats of wild animals. Tourists must exercise caution and remain alert during their visit.

  • Do not get down from safari vehicles inside forest zones
  • Avoid loud noises, shouting, or playing music
  • Do not provoke, feed, or attempt to approach animals
  • Photography without flash only
  • Follow emergency instructions provided by guides

Seasonal Travel Advisory

Wildlife tourism in India is seasonal and depends on weather conditions and breeding cycles of animals.

  • Summer (March–June): High wildlife sightings, heat precautions required
  • Monsoon (July–September): Many parks closed due to safety reasons
  • Winter (October–February): Best time for tourism in most regions

Safari-Specific Advisory

  • Safari bookings should be made in advance through official portals
  • Limited safari vehicles allowed per zone per day
  • Morning and evening safaris operate as per park rules
  • Safari permits are non-transferable
  • Violation of safari rules may lead to penalties

Environmental Responsibility

Visitors are expected to support conservation efforts by adopting eco-friendly practices during their visit.

  • Plastic-free forest zones – carry reusable items
  • Do not litter inside forest areas
  • Respect local communities and traditions
  • Report illegal activities to forest officials

Health & Medical Advisory

  • Carry basic first-aid and prescribed medicines
  • Stay hydrated during safari tours
  • Wear appropriate clothing and footwear
  • Follow COVID or health advisories if applicable

Legal Notice

Wildlife tourism in India is governed by the Wildlife Protection Act. Any violation of forest rules, wildlife disturbance, or illegal activity is punishable under applicable laws.
