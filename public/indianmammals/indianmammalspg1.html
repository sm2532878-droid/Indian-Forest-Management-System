Indian Mammals

Authoritative information on mammal species found across India

India is one of the world’s 17 mega-diverse countries, supporting more than 420 species of mammals. These mammals inhabit a wide range of ecosystems including tropical rainforests, grasslands, deserts, wetlands, mangroves, and alpine regions of the Himalayas.

Indian mammals play a crucial role in ecosystem stability, seed dispersal, predator-prey balance, and climate regulation. Many species are protected under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972.

Scientific Classification of Indian Mammals

  • Carnivora: Tigers, Lions, Leopards, Bears
  • Proboscidea: Elephants
  • Perissodactyla: Rhinoceros
  • Artiodactyla: Deer, Antelope
  • Primates: Langurs, Macaques
  • Chiroptera: Bats (largest group)
  • Rodentia: Rats, Squirrels, Porcupines
  • Cetacea: Dolphins & Whales
  • Sirenia: Dugong
