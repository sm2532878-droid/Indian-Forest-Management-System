Safari Guidelines & Visitor Rules

Responsible tourism helps protect wildlife, preserve forests and ensure safe safari experiences for every visitor.

Essential Safari Guidelines

Every visitor entering National Parks or Tiger Reserves must follow Forest Department regulations.

🐅 Wildlife Protection Rules

  • Do not feed or disturb wild animals.
  • Maintain silence during safari.
  • Never throw plastic or waste inside forests.
  • Do not chase animals for photography.
  • Protect natural habitats and biodiversity.

🚙 Safari Vehicle Rules

  • Remain seated inside safari vehicles.
  • Do not get down from the jeep.
  • Follow guide and driver instructions.
  • Over-speeding inside forest areas is prohibited.
  • Only authorized safari vehicles are allowed.

📷 Tourist Guidelines

  • Photography is allowed without disturbing wildlife.
  • Carry valid ID proof during safari.
  • Avoid loud music and shouting.
  • Wear comfortable and natural-colored clothes.
  • Respect local forest communities.

⚠ Safety Instructions

  • Wild animals are unpredictable.
  • Children should stay with guardians.
  • Smoking and alcohol are strictly prohibited.
  • Follow emergency instructions immediately.
  • Report unsafe activity to forest officials.

Important Forest Department Notice

Safari permits may be cancelled due to weather conditions, forest emergencies, wildlife movement or government orders. Visitors are requested to cooperate with forest officials and support responsible eco-tourism.
